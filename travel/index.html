Questions

What contracting teams ask us.

Do we have to replace our booking engine?

No. Lumif.ai sits beside the booking engine you run today and loads into it via API or a configured integration. Your booking engine stays the system of record.

What happens when a document is unclear or contradicts itself?

It doesn't guess. The update waits for your team with both sources shown, and nothing contested loads until someone decides.

How do we know what loaded is right?

Every rate is checked against the contract and what's live before it loads, then read back from your booking engine after. Every live rate links back to the clause it came from.

Is our data used to train models?

No. Storage is isolated and encrypted, every action is kept on an append-only trail, and Lumif.ai is SOC 2 Type 2 audited.
